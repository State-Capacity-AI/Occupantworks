What I help with
A second read
The original "phone a friend." You need someone with range to look at something and tell you what they see. A vendor proposal, an architecture decision, a hiring plan, a strategy deck. I've given this kind of counsel to agency directors, CIOs, and city managers. Sometimes the most valuable thing is someone who's seen it before saying "this is fine" or "don't do that."

Standing up digital capacity
You want to build an internal team but don't know where to start. I've done this at federal scale and at community scale with no budget at all. I can help you scope what you actually need, write the roles, structure the team, and avoid the mistakes that burn through your first year.

Training and teaching
I teach service design and public mechanics at a research university. I can run workshops, brief your leadership team, or train your staff on service delivery, procurement literacy, AI governance, or how to think about the systems they're inheriting. I explain complex things clearly because that's literally my job.

Delivery and deployment
Something is stalled, sideways, or about to go wrong. I've led delivery for work touching billions in federal funding, redesigned city services end-to-end, and built prototypes for military field logistics. I can diagnose what's broken, get it unstuck, and help your team finish.

Procurement and vendor strategy
You're about to sign something expensive and you're not sure it's right. I've operated inside the federal procurement system, managed vendor relationships for city governments, and built the tools that help agencies evaluate what they're buying. I can read the contract and tell you where the risk is.

AI and technology strategy
You're hearing about AI from vendors and leadership and you need someone who understands both the technology and the governance. I run a research lab on AI and state capacity. I've built decision transparency prototypes and benefits determination systems. I can help you figure out what's real, what's hype, and what your actual obligations are.
